# Acceptable Use Policy

> SYNTHETIC: for demonstration only

## Purpose

Example Organization systems are provided for authorized work. This sample policy describes safe and respectful use by users and service providers.

## User expectations

- Use only accounts and systems assigned or explicitly approved for you.
- Keep authentication factors private and use multifactor authentication when available.
- Store work information only in approved locations and follow its classification.
- Lock unattended devices and promptly install approved updates.
- Report suspicious messages, unexpected access prompts, lost devices, or accidental disclosures to the Security Lead.

## Prohibited use

Users must not share accounts, bypass security controls, install unapproved software, copy restricted information to personal services, or use organization systems to access or distribute unlawful, harassing, or malicious content.

Security testing is allowed only when the Security Lead has approved the scope, schedule, and responsible personnel in writing. This policy does not authorize testing of third-party systems.

## Monitoring and consequences

Example Organization may review system activity for security, operational, and policy purposes, subject to applicable rules and notice. Reported issues are reviewed fairly; corrective action is proportionate to the circumstances and may include access changes or training.

## Acknowledgment

Users confirm that they understand this policy and know how to report a security concern before receiving access.
