## Due diligence questions

1. What information will the vendor access, process, store, or transmit?
2. Which locations and subcontractors are involved?
3. How are identities, privileged access, and authentication protected?
4. How are data encrypted, backed up, retained, and securely deleted?
5. How are security events detected, investigated, and communicated?
6. How are vulnerabilities and software changes managed?
7. What independent assurance reports or certifications are available, and what are their scope and dates?
8. How can Example Organization retrieve its information and end the service safely?

## Review and decision

The reviewer records each answer as Satisfactory, Follow-up Required, or Not Applicable, with evidence references and open questions. A high-risk gap requires an owner, mitigation, and due date before approval.

The business owner and Security Lead record one outcome: Approved, Approved with Conditions, or Not Approved. Conditions include an expiry or reassessment date. Reassess after a material service change, significant incident, or contract renewal.

This fictional template is a starting point, not a certification or legal determination.
