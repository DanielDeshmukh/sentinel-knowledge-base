# CERT-In AI Security Auditing Guidelines: Overview

> Informational summary only. This overview describes the published guidance; it is not legal advice or an audit result.

## Source and scope

CERT-In's official PDF identifies itself as "Artificial Intelligence (AI) Security Auditing Guidelines," version 1.0, dated 4 November 2026. It presents a risk-based approach for assessing AI systems. This summary covers that document and does not make claims about other audit policies that are not cited here.

## Main ideas

- Classify each AI system into one of four risk tiers: Low, Moderate, High, or Critical.
- Scale assessment depth, independence, and audit frequency with the assigned tier and system context.
- Reassess the tier periodically and when material changes or risk triggers occur.
- Keep evidence for governance, data handling, model evaluation, access, logging, monitoring, human oversight, and incident response.
- Plan for AI-related incidents and forensic readiness. The guideline refers to CERT-In incident timelines and log-retention requirements; those requirements are set out in the separate 2022 Directions.

The guideline is a framework for AI security audits. Its publication should not be read as changing the scope or legal status of the 2022 Directions.
