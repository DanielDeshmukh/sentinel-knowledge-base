# Digital Personal Data Protection Readiness Checklist

> SYNTHETIC: for demonstration only

## Inventory and accountability

- [ ] Record personal-data categories, purposes, systems, recipients, and retention owners.
- [ ] Identify the responsible Data Fiduciary and relevant Data Processors for each activity.
- [ ] Document which provisions and commencement dates may apply; verify them against current official notifications.

## Notices and rights

- [ ] Review notices for clear descriptions of personal data and processing purposes.
- [ ] Define a workflow to receive, verify, route, and answer Data Principal requests.
- [ ] Publish an appropriate contact route and track response time and resolution.

## Security and incidents

- [ ] Restrict access and review permissions for systems containing personal data.
- [ ] Maintain safeguards, backups, access records, and tested response procedures.
- [ ] Define how the organization assesses a possible breach and who decides external notifications.
- [ ] Review Data Fiduciary-Data Processor arrangements and incident escalation paths.

## Retention and assurance

- [ ] Document retention purposes, deletion triggers, legal holds, and backup expiry.
- [ ] Review whether additional Data Fiduciary obligations or assessments apply.
- [ ] Record evidence, owners, gaps, and target dates; reassess after relevant legal or system changes.

This checklist is a fictional planning aid, not a legal opinion or a determination that any requirement applies. Consult the Act, Rules, current notifications, and qualified counsel.
