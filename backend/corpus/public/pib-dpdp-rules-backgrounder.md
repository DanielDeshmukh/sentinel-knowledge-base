# PIB Backgrounder on the DPDP Rules, 2025

## What this source is

The Press Information Bureau published a backgrounder titled "DPDP Rules, 2025 Notified" on 17 November 2025. It is a government explainer that summarizes the Act and Rules and links to their full texts; it is not a substitute for the Gazette notification.

## How to use it

The backgrounder is useful for orientation: it describes the purpose of the Rules, public consultation, privacy-related roles, and the intended relationship between individual rights and organizational duties. Its date statement differs from the Gazette text, which is dated 13 November 2025. Use the Gazette when recording the notification date or interpreting a legal obligation.

The backgrounder also describes compliance timing and selected requirements in plain language. Verify each such detail against the Rule itself before using it in a policy, assessment, or legal decision.
