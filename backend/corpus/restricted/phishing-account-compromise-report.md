# Account Compromise Incident Report

> SYNTHETIC: for demonstration only

## Summary

Example Organization detected suspicious sign-in activity for a fictional support account after the account owner interacted with an unexpected authentication prompt. The case is closed for training purposes; no real user, tenant, or service is involved.

## Timeline

| Relative time | Event |
| --- | --- |

| 12:20 | The account was restored with new authentication factors and additional monitoring |

## Response

The response team preserved relevant authentication logs, checked recent account changes, reviewed the account's assigned permissions, and confirmed that recovery factors were controlled by the account owner. The team also searched for similar alerts across the same training environment.

## Follow-up

- Require phishing-resistant multifactor authentication for privileged support access.
- Improve reporting instructions for unexpected authentication prompts.
- Review sign-in alert thresholds and account recovery procedures.

All times, roles, and events in this report are invented.
