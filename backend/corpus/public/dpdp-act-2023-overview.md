# Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023: Overview

> Informational summary only; not legal advice.

## Purpose

The Act provides a framework for processing digital personal data for lawful purposes while recognizing the interests of individuals in protecting their data. It defines roles such as Data Principal, Data Fiduciary, and Data Processor, and establishes the Data Protection Board of India.

## Practical themes

- A Data Fiduciary is accountable for deciding why and how personal data is processed.
- Processing is based on consent or another use permitted by the Act. A notice explains the data and purpose involved.
- Data Fiduciaries have duties that include reasonable security safeguards, breach handling, and responding to rights requests.
- Data Principals have rights that include access to information about processing, correction or erasure in applicable circumstances, grievance redressal, and nominating another person to exercise rights.
- The Act provides for monetary penalties; the applicable provision and circumstances matter.

## Commencement

Section 1 allows the Central Government to appoint commencement dates by Official Gazette notification, including different dates for different provisions. Do not assume every provision began on the date the Act was enacted.
