# CERT-In Directions on Cyber Incident Reporting

> Informational summary only; check the official Directions for scope and legal interpretation.

## Source

The CERT-In Directions under section 70B(6) of the Information Technology Act are dated 28 April 2022. They apply to the entities named in the document and specify incident reporting, recordkeeping, time synchronization, and a point of contact.

## Operational points

- Covered entities must report incidents listed in Annexure I to CERT-In within six hours of noticing them or being informed of them.
- Covered entities must enable ICT-system logs, retain them securely for a rolling 180 days, and keep them within Indian jurisdiction.
- Clocks for ICT systems are to be synchronized with the specified Indian time sources or qualifying traceable sources.
- Covered entities must designate and keep current a point of contact for CERT-In.

The reporting requirement is tied to the listed incident types and entities in the Directions; do not treat it as a blanket statement that every security event is reportable under this instrument.
