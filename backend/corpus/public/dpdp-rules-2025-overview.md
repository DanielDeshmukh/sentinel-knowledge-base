# Digital Personal Data Protection Rules, 2025: Overview

> Informational summary only; not legal advice. Check the Gazette text and later notifications before relying on a commencement date or duty.

## Source and date

The Gazette text is G.S.R. 846(E), dated 13 November 2025. A PIB backgrounder posted on 17 November says the Rules were notified on 14 November. This summary uses the Gazette for the legal text and preserves the date discrepancy rather than treating the backgrounder as the controlling source.

## Phased commencement

Rule 1 sets different start points: Rules 1, 2, and 17 to 21 commence on Gazette publication; Rule 4 commences one year later; Rules 3, 5 to 16, 22, and 23 commence eighteen months later. Confirm the relevant notification and provision before applying these dates to a real organization.

## Security safeguards and breach notices

Rule 6 describes safeguards that include protecting data through measures such as encryption, masking, or tokens; controlling access; logging, monitoring, and reviewing access; maintaining backups; and retaining specified logs and personal data for one year, unless another law requires otherwise. It also addresses safeguards in Data Fiduciary-Data Processor arrangements.

Rule 7 calls for affected Data Principals to be informed without delay in clear language. The Data Protection Board is to be informed without delay, with detailed and updated information within seventy-two hours of awareness unless a longer period is allowed.

## Use carefully

This short summary does not decide which provisions apply to a particular entity or how they interact with other laws. Review the Gazette text and current government notifications for operational decisions.
