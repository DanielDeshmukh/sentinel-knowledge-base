# ISO/IEC 27001:2022: Original Overview

## What it is

ISO/IEC 27001:2022 specifies requirements for establishing, implementing, maintaining, and continually improving an Information Security Management System (ISMS). The standard describes a risk-management approach that organizations can tailor to their context.

An ISMS connects people, processes, and technology. Its purpose is to manage information-security risks and support appropriate confidentiality, integrity, and availability of information.
