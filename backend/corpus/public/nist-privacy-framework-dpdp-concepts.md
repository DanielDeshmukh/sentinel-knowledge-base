# NIST Privacy Framework and DPDP: Conceptual Comparison

> Informational comparison only; not legal advice, a compliance crosswalk, or a determination of equivalence.

## Different kinds of instruments

The NIST Privacy Framework is a voluntary risk-management tool intended to help organizations identify and manage privacy risk. India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act and Rules are legal instruments with duties and rights that depend on their text, commencement, and applicability.

## A practical mapping method

An organization can use a privacy-risk process to organize work that also supports DPDP readiness:

1. Inventory personal-data flows and identify the purposes and roles involved.
2. Assign accountable owners and document the legal basis, notices, and decisions.
3. Implement controls for access, retention, security, rights requests, and incident handling.
4. Explain data practices to individuals and provide channels for questions or requests.
5. Reassess the controls when processing, systems, or applicable rules change.

These are planning prompts, not a clause-by-clause mapping. A NIST framework profile does not establish compliance with Indian law, and legal requirements should be checked against the Act, Rules, and official notifications.

## Version note

At retrieval, NIST's Privacy Framework page highlights a version 1.1 Initial Public Draft. Confirm the version and status of any NIST material used in an assessment.
