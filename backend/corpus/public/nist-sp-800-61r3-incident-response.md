# NIST SP 800-61 Revision 3: Incident Response

## Overview

NIST Special Publication 800-61 Revision 3, published in April 2025, places incident-response recommendations within the broader cybersecurity risk-management approach of the NIST Cybersecurity Framework 2.0. It is intended to help organizations prepare for incidents, reduce their likelihood and impact, and improve detection, response, and recovery.

## Practical application

- Treat incident response as an ongoing organizational capability, not a task that begins only after an alert.
- Assign responsibilities and prepare communications, evidence handling, and decision paths before an event.
- Connect detection and analysis to coordinated response and recovery.
- Review outcomes after incidents and feed lessons into risk management and preparedness.

The publication is U.S. federal guidance. Organizations elsewhere can adapt its risk-management ideas, but it does not replace local legal or regulatory requirements.
