# Information Security Policy

> SYNTHETIC: for demonstration only

## Purpose and scope

Example Organization protects the information it creates, receives, and processes. This sample policy applies to personnel, temporary workers, and service providers who access the organization's systems or information.

## Responsibilities

- Leadership approves the security program and accepts documented residual risk.
- The Security Lead maintains this policy, coordinates risk reviews, and tracks corrective work.
- System Owners define access, retention, and recovery requirements for their services.
- Users protect their credentials, complete required training, and report suspected incidents promptly.

## Minimum safeguards

Example Organization uses unique user accounts, least-privilege access, multifactor authentication for important systems, supported software, protected backups, and monitoring appropriate to system risk. Restricted information is shared only through approved channels and only with authorized recipients.

System Owners review access and recovery arrangements periodically. Changes that affect sensitive information or critical services receive a documented security review before release.

## Incident handling

Users report suspected loss, disclosure, or unauthorized access to the Security Lead through the organization's approved internal channel. The response team records the event, protects relevant evidence, limits further exposure, restores services safely, and tracks follow-up actions.

## Exceptions and review

Exceptions require a named business owner, documented reason, compensating safeguards, expiry date, and approval from the Security Lead. Review this policy at least annually and after material changes.
