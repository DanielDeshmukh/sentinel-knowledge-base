# Data Classification and Handling Standard

> SYNTHETIC: for demonstration only

## Classification levels

Example Organization classifies information according to its likely impact if disclosed, changed, or unavailable.

| Class | Example | Minimum handling |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Public | Approved external material | May be shared publicly after owner approval |
| Internal | Routine non-public work information | Store in approved work systems and share with authorized users |
| Restricted | Sensitive records, credentials, or security investigations | Limit access by role, use approved protected storage and transfer, and log access where supported |

## Handling rules

The information owner assigns a class when information is created or received. When classification is uncertain, treat the information as Restricted until the owner decides otherwise.

Users share the minimum information needed for a task. Restricted information must not be copied to personal devices, unapproved storage, or public collaboration spaces. A recipient who no longer needs access must return or securely delete their copy under the owner's instructions.

## Retention and disposal

System Owners document the business retention period and any applicable legal hold. At the end of the approved period, information is securely deleted or de-identified using the system's supported process. Backups and replicas follow a documented expiry schedule.

## Incident reporting

Suspected misclassification, loss, or unauthorized disclosure is reported promptly to the Security Lead. Preserve relevant records and do not attempt to conceal or independently investigate a suspected breach.
