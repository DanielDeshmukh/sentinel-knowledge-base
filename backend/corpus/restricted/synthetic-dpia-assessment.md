# Synthetic DPIA-Style Assessment

> SYNTHETIC: for demonstration only

## Proposed processing

Example Organization proposes a fictional customer-support platform that stores account preferences and support requests. The exercise assumes that a Data Fiduciary determines the service purpose and a Data Processor operates part of the platform. No real individual or personal record is represented here.

## Necessity and proportionality prompts

- Is each data field needed for the stated support purpose?
- Is the purpose explained before collection, and can it be changed without review?
- Are access, retention, and deletion rules defined for each system?
- Can a person use the service without unnecessary data collection?
- Are processors and service integrations limited to the approved purpose?

## Risks and proposed safeguards

| Risk | Potential effect | Example safeguard |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Excessive staff access | Unnecessary disclosure | Role-based access, periodic review, and audit records |
| Account compromise | Unauthorized viewing or changes | Multifactor authentication, session controls, and alerting |
| Retention exceeds the purpose | Prolonged exposure | Documented deletion schedules and verified expiry |
| Processor incident is reported late | Delayed containment | Contracted escalation route and joint response exercise |

## Residual risk and review

The fictional owner records any unresolved risk, acceptance authority, and review date before launch. Reassess after a material purpose, data, vendor, or system change. This exercise is not a legal determination or a completed assessment of a real service.
